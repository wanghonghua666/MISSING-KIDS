About

Luna is a contemporary artist based in New York, specializing in abstract and mixed-media works that explore the boundaries between reality and imagination. With over a decade of experience, her work has been featured in galleries and exhibitions across the country.

Her artistic practice is driven by a fascination with color theory, geometric forms, and the emotional resonance of visual composition. Each piece is meticulously crafted to evoke contemplation and invite viewers into a dialogue with the artwork.

When not in the studio, Luna can be found teaching art workshops and mentoring emerging artists in the community.
